Why the matches are bad, not yet confirmed:
- LightGlue's SuperPoint resizes every image so its longer side is 1024 px, and it upscales small
  images too (lightglue/superpoint.py:116 "resize": 1024, applied in utils.py:143 extract). [code]
  So the 90x90 glasses crop is blown up about 11 times and the wrist crop about 4.5 times: blurry,
  and at two different scales. That fits SuperPoint reporting 2048 keypoints on a 90x90 crop,
  more than such a small image can hold at its 4 px spacing. [inferred]
- A plain box has few features, and its corners look alike, so the matcher cannot tell one
  corner from another. [inferred]
