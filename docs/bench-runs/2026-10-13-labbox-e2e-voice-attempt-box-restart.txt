  3. Third try: six panes sourced grasp/grasp_env.sh. In the clone grasp/anygrasp_venv/.venv did
     not exist (gitignored, not linked), so each pane started building it (MinkowskiEngine compile).
     Load average 54, SSH stopped answering, Dion restarted the box.
  Also found before the third try: three audio workers left from the earlier T2.6 replays, each
  holding 1.4 GB of GPU. Cause: `timeout` sent SIGTERM to the replay tool, which skips its cleanup.
  Stopped by their own process groups. Use `timeout -s INT` with the replay tool.

Lessons
  - Before running from a scratch clone, read what its env files build or install. Link every
    gitignored folder they look for (grasp/anygrasp_venv, assets/models), or use the lightest env.
  - Stop only your own process groups. Leave anyone else's to Dion.
