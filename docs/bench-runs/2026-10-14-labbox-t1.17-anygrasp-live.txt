=== Step 7d, 2026-10-14 evening: the first AnyGrasp grasp on the real arm ===
Box ~/rcp-Gappler on dev c854d07 plus the depth fix copied in (uncommitted) and the local edit
use_simple_execute false. ./quickstart_terminals.sh grasp --anygrasp, AnyGrasp restarted with the
fix, state machine started by Dion, one /manipulator/grasp_request typed. Box at 44.5 cm, 6 cm face
forward. Pane logs on the box: ~/dion_logs/2026-10-14_t1.17_anygrasp/.

Result: FAIL, box knocked off. SELECTING approached the box to 0.209 m, EXECUTING, gripper opened,
"pose stable, locking target", MtcPlanner planned to x -0.580 y -0.024 z 0.237 (base_link),
executed in 9 s, "Cartesian move succeeded", gripper closed, then the return.
Seen by Dion: the approach looked right, but right before closing the wrist turned about 180 deg
and the jaws knocked the box off the table.

Cause [inferred, the wrist joint was not recorded]: the gripper is symmetric, so a grasp and the
same grasp turned 180 deg about the approach are equal, and AnyGrasp picks either (the sign of its
closing axis is arbitrary). The state machine sends the orientation unchanged. The final move is
a planned joint move (moveToPose, grasp_state_machine.cpp:655, "Planning to pose"), not a straight
line, so the wrist is free to turn the long way next to the box.
Fix proposed (T1.33, not built): before the final move, if the target's closing axis points
opposite to the gripper's current one, turn the target 180 deg about its approach.
