2026-10-14 lab box: AnyGrasp on the live wrist camera, arm still (T1.17, step 7a)

Why: on the replay, AnyGrasp's grasps looked impossible (Dion), scores 0.007 to 0.036
(bench-runs/2026-10-14-labbox-t1.14-t1.17-sim-and-replay.txt). Find the cause before the arm
moves to an AnyGrasp grasp.

Setup: branch t1.8-t1.10-anygrasp-gate-and-pane at 296cc64, ROS domain 97, localhost only. Wrist
camera (640x480x15), segmentation service (SAM 3), grasp segmentation client (default prompt
"box"). No arm driver, no state machine, no anygrasp_detection_node: nothing could move. A
scratch tool (~/dion_t1.17_live/live_anygrasp_check.py on the box, not in the repo) ran its own
AnyGrasp with the node's settings on the same topics, drew the top 5 grasps live on the box's
screen, and logged each set (~/dion_t1.17_live/<time>/sets.csv). Box about 0.4 m from the camera,
arm at HOME. Box: 6 cm x 7.5 cm x 14 cm high. The camera sees the front face and the top face.

Modes the tool switched between:
  node    box points only (SAM mask), wide limits, collision check on: what the node does
  scene   every point within 10 cm of the box, tight limits, collision on, grasps kept only if
          their centre lands on the mask
  nocoll  node, collision check off
  tight   node, but the scene mode's tight limits
  f       filter: keep grasps whose approach is within 30 deg of the camera's view

1. Gripper model 0.07 m, as the node had it (cfgs.max_gripper_width, anygrasp_detection_node.py:34)
   mode    frames  with grasps  top score (median)  approach (median)
   node      94        59           0.007               89 deg
   scene     36         9           0.043               55 deg
   nocoll   117        95           0.005               89 deg
   Points per frame: 15,800 on the box, 90,300 in scene mode. So "too few points" is ruled out.
   node = nocoll, so the collision check against the mat is ruled out. Dion: the grasps go for
   the box's right face, which the camera cannot see, some from the left side through the box.

2. Depth inside the mask, one frame: 15,781 valid points, 0.38 to 0.48 m, continuous (front face
   plus the top face seen from above). 157 stray points at 0.63 m. No sheet of points behind the
   box. Mask 93 px wide at 0.40 m, about 6.1 cm: the box's 6 cm face.

3. Gripper model 0.10 m (same frames, same box)
   mode      f   frames  with grasps  top score (median)  approach (median)
   node      off   401       401          0.505               52 deg
   node      on    253       253          0.367               23 deg
   scene     on     31        31          0.387               10 deg
   nocoll    on    112       112          0.573               26 deg
   tight     on     55        55          0.367               23 deg
   Dion: much better, "finding the obvious front face grasp". node off still has some diagonals
   across the top face, tight the most diagonals, nocoll the best.
   The "f on" rows of this run came from a tool version that kept the SDK's grasp objects while
   iterating, and a few logged angles were over 30 deg. Fixed in the next run (copied the values
   out): approach max 30 deg with f on.

4. AnyGrasp's width (logged from run 4 on): every grasp is reported at exactly the model width
   (0.100 on both faces). AnyGrasp does not predict the opening, it checks a gripper open to the
   model width fits around the object. So told 0.07, a 6 cm face leaves 5 mm a side, too little
   for the depth noise at the box's edges [inferred]. That is the cause of the impossible grasps:
   the obvious grasp was rejected, leaving only odd ones.

5. Gripper model 0.08 m, both faces, f on
   6 cm face:   58% of 135 frames have a grasp, top score 0.143 (0.10 m gave 100%, 0.344)
   7.5 cm face: 3 of 5 frames (f was mostly off then, too few to judge)
   0.08 does not separate "fits in the real 71 mm gripper" from "does not": the good face loses
   half its grasps. Not used.

Decision (Dion): max_gripper_width_m 0.10 and max_approach_deg 30 in grasp/grasp_config.yaml,
the node drops grasps beyond 30 deg. 30 deg is a first-run safety limit, not measured: the
candidate path moves to the grasp's full pose (moveToPose, grasp_state_machine.cpp:660), so
larger angles may work. The object-fits-in-71-mm check is T1.32. Until then only place the box
in an orientation the gripper can close on.

Found while reading the candidate path [unverified]: the state machine's own angle check,
min_approach_angle_deg 20 (grasp_state_machine.cpp:597), computes 1 - 2(qx^2 + qy^2) (:82-86),
the z component of the grasp's z axis. AnyGrasp's approach is its x axis. Check in RViz with the
first real candidate (T1.17), together with grasp_frame.

Not tested: anything with the arm or the state machine. The node with the new settings (only the
scratch tool ran them). The candidate path starts AnyGrasp in EXECUTING at about 0.21 m from the
box, not 0.4 m (T1.17 risk).
