3. Speech-to-text latency (review guide section 7 item 1), domain 89
   Scratch script ~/dion_t2.9/stt_latency.py: plays pick_up_box_command.wav into the real
   audio_worker (Whisper + LLM on the GPU), each 0.25 s chunk handed over at its real time, as
   the live AudioObserver does. Times the word's arrival on /aria/audio/prompt from the end of
   speech. The voice detector finds speech from 1.63 s to the file's end at 4.29 s, so the
   recording stops right at the last word: the true end of speech is at or before 4.29 s, and
   the true latency at least what is shown.
   5 plays: 1.32 s (first, warm-up), 0.62, 0.65, 0.65, 0.65 s. Median 0.65 s. Word 'box' each time.
   Not measured: the glasses' own audio streaming delay, which happens before audio_worker.
   CORRECTION (same evening): the recording is noisy (loudness floor about 0.05 of peak), so the
   voice detector ran its span to the file's end. Loudness in 0.1 s steps shows the last speech
   peak at about 3.3 s, so speech ends near 3.4 s [inferred], and the latency is about
   0.65 + 0.9 = 1.5 s. That fits CHANNEL_CONTRACT A-5: 1.1 s by design (0.7 s quiet, 0.4 s
   padding) plus Whisper and the LLM. 0.65 s is a lower bound only.
   Result: Dion chose the knob, speech_latency_s 0.65 in aria_config.yaml, before the correction. Client tests after
   it: 22 pass (16 client plus 6 gaze pick).
