  Over-merging shows as misses, not false merges: with phys_bias 0 or threshold 0.3 the store
  collapses to 34 to 36 objects and 10 to 14 of the 16 real objects are missed. A merged blob
  spreads its points over many real objects, so none holds the 15 percent the false-merge rule
  needs. Read the misses column as the over-merge signal.

  Radius, phys_bias and threshold together (real gate, 36 runs), the stable region:
    radius 15 to 20 cm with phys_bias 0.5 or 0.75 and threshold 0.5 to 0.7: 2 or 3 duplicates,
    0 false merges, 3 misses, 13 to 14 extras. phys_bias 0.25 with threshold 0.7 gives 1 or 2
    duplicates, but phys_bias 0.25 with threshold 0.6 already collapses (5 to 7 misses), so it
    sits next to a cliff. Best five rechecked at the fine gate: radius 20 cm / bias 0.25 /
    threshold 0.7 gives 1 duplicate, 3 misses; radius 15 cm / 0.25 / 0.7 the same; radius
    20 cm / 0.5 / 0.5 gives 2 duplicates, 3 misses.

  Reading (for D-MG2 and D-MG8):
    - The match radius is the lever. Upstream's 2.5 cm is too tight for this depth: sparse
      LiDAR and two views of one object 4 to 9 cm apart (T6.3). 10 to 20 cm cuts duplicates
      from 8 to 2 or 3 with no false merges and no new misses, at both gates.
    - Giving appearance more weight (phys_bias down) does not fix duplicates safely: it merges
      everything that touches. Geometry-dominant stays right (D-MG2).
    - Greedy beats one_to_one (8 against 13 duplicates). D-MG8 stays greedy.
    - min_points 32 helps a little (5 duplicates) by dropping small sightings, at the cost of
      small objects. Not recommended alone.
  Limits: one drive, 16 real objects, 8 baseline duplicates. Differences of 1 are noise.
