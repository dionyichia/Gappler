Step 2, scoring rule settled (Zongzhe, same day)
  Parts: the glass bookcase (gt 2) is flagged many_parts in the ground truth file, so its
  per-cell "shelf" objects count as parts. Everywhere else every extra same-kind object is a
  duplicate. Two automatic rules were tried first and rejected: by size (graph boxes are much
  smaller than marked ones, so chair 13's 4 copies became parts) and by touching points
  (neighbouring bookcase cells share boards, so the cells became duplicates). Wide duplicates
  across labels were dropped: other labels on a real object are reported as other_label.
