Findings
- 600 of 602 published centroids on the looked-at box over runs 3 to 5. The old pick: wrong box in
  114 of 114 frames on the same cardboard scene. [measured]
- A wrong reference is not always caught. With the gaze on the mat (1.5 m, T2.3), the CLIP cutoff
  refused every frame in run 4 but let 2 frames through in run 5. With a grasp request open, the
  arm would take the wrong box. Raising the cutoff does not fix it: in word 3 the right match was
  only 0.579. The fix is to refuse weak gaze picks before the Target goes out: T2.24, next, before
  any arm test with the glasses. [measured]
- The 0.01 tie margin did its job in word 3 (run 4): two boxes 0.004 apart were refused, not guessed.
  Wider margins would refuse more frames here without changing any pick. [measured]
