Findings, parked (tasks in the task tree)
  1. The grip closes a few cm short every time (Dion). Same as step 4's "3 cm short": the mask and
     its depth are the box's front face. A longer final step would fail sooner at the far spots,
     so this pulls against the reach limit. T1.17 (AnyGrasp) is the planned fix.
  2. No grasp angle. The jaws always close at the same yaw, so Dion turned the box to face the arm
     for 3 and 4. AnyGrasp chooses an angle (T1.17).
  3. The replay reloads Whisper and the LLM each run, about a minute an attempt. Fixed on branch
     t2.16-replay-load-once (T2.16).
  4. MoveIt warns "The computed trajectory is too short to detect jumps in joint-space Need at
     least 10 steps, only got 3. Try a lower max_step." on every approach step (move_group, pane_1).
     The steps are about 3 cm and computeCartesianPath splits them at 1 cm (mtc_planner.cpp:117), so
     the check for a sudden joint flip never runs [code]. A smaller step (0.002 m) would turn it on.
  5. Two stale-centroid misses (step 5 #7, step 6 first try), both early in SELECTING. The rate and
     delay looked normal before and after. Not known whether SAM 3 lost the box, the mask had no
     valid depth (grasp_segmentation_client.py:145, the cause in step 4), or the hot CPU stalled
     [unverified].
  6. /pipeline_state shows EXECUTING the whole time the arm holds the box. There is no holding state:
     nothing is published between EXECUTING (grasp_state_machine.cpp:801) and IDLE (:884) on the
     t1.14 branch [code].
  7. The hand-release command opens the gripper behind the state machine's back. Use only the voice
     release with T1.14 in, and keep the manual command out of the robot session's panes.
