## 1. What the branch does, in five lines

1. When you say a word, the aria client still sends the word at once (unchanged). Then it segments
   the newest glasses image with that word, picks the mask you look at, and sends a second Target
   with that object's crop and mask as its reference.
2. Gaze is averaged over 1 s. The pick is the mask under it, else the nearest within 80 px.
3. `et_worker` now publishes gaze in the pixels of the published (undistorted, rotated) glasses
   image, so every reader compares it with masks directly.
4. T2.13: the glasses image is undistorted before it is rotated. It was the other way round,
   about 12.5 px off.
5. The segmentation service loads at cutoff 0.3 and 20 detections, because the glasses need many
   weak boxes to choose from. The grasp client asks for its old 0.5 and 5.

## 5. Open problem: the word at 22 s finds no glasses image

**What happens** [measured]: in 3 of 4 runs the client logs `No glasses image: the word alone is
sent` at 22 s. A logger on `/aria/rgb_camera/undistorted` saw no image at all in a 30 s replay,
while gaze started about 12 s in. In full runs, images do arrive later, one every 2 to 3 s.

**Most likely cause** [inferred, found while writing this]: the replay's readiness check does not
work. It puts one frame on each worker's queue and waits until `queue.empty()`.
`multiprocessing.Queue.put` hands the item to a background thread, and `empty()` only looks at the
pipe. So `empty()` can be True before the frame was ever sent, and the replay starts at once. Then
`rgb_worker` is still building its undistortion maps (`undistortion_helper.py:7-18`, about 2 million
per-pixel Python calls, 20 s or more on the throttled box) while the recording's first 20 to 30 s
play. Frames that arrive then are replaced in the size-1 queue and lost. The fixed 30 s wait
worked once and failed once, which fits a map build that takes about 30 s under load.

**How to check it** (about 10 minutes, box idle first):

1. Add one timestamped print in `rgb_worker` after `build_remap_maps` (`image_streaming_pipeline.py`,
   after the maps line), and one in the replay at "Playing from".
2. Run the 30 s replay with the logger (`~/dion_t2.9/arrivals.py`, run from `aria/aria_app` with
   `aria_env.sh` sourced). If "maps ready" comes after "Playing", this is the cause.

**Fix options, if confirmed:**

| Option | Cost | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Replay waits for the first message on `/aria/rgb_camera/undistorted` and on the gaze topic | about 10 lines in the replay | Fixes the test only |
| Build the maps faster: vectorise `build_remap_maps`, or cache them on disk per calibration | small to medium | Also helps live, where the first image arrives 20+ s after start |

Either way the box is slow: the kernel's `idle_inject` threads took 50 % of every core during the
runs, which is how it caps CPU use, usually for heat [measured in top, cause inferred]. That is T2.15.

## 7. Checks to do after the review

1. **Speech-to-text latency** (found in review, Dion 2026-10-14). **Measured 2026-10-15**: the word
   arrives 0.65 s after the speech ends (median of 5, the first 1.3 s while warming up), from the
   recorded `pick_up_box` command played into the real `audio_worker`. At least that much, since
   the recording ends on the last word. The glasses' own audio delay is not included. Over the
   0.5 s line below, so Dion chose the knob: `speech_latency_s: 0.65` in `aria_config.yaml`,
   the gaze window ends that far before the word arrived. One new test, 22 pass on the box.
   Original note: The gaze window ends when the
   word *arrives* (`aria_segmentation_client.py:120`), not when it was spoken, and the prompt is
   a `String` with no stamp. The e2e script publishes the word straight onto the topic, so it
   skips speech-to-text, and the latency was never measured [inferred]. The image wait makes it
   later still: with a stale image, gaze is averaged 0.5 to 1.5 s after arrival (lesson 4).
   - Measure it first. Under about 0.5 s: no change.
   - Larger: a config knob `speech_latency_s` that ends the gaze window that far back (about 3
     lines). If it varies a lot: a stamped prompt message (a contract change).
   - Add a task to the task tree only if a change is needed.
2. **Stale reply with the same word** (found in review, Dion 2026-10-14). **Done 2026-10-15**:
   `self.words` counts every word, `segment` captures it, `on_reply` drops the reply if it moved.
   New test `test_a_late_reply_after_the_same_word_again_is_dropped`. Compiles and bench L0-L2
   pass on the Mac. Box 2026-10-15: 21 tests pass (15 client plus 6 gaze pick)
   (`docs/bench-runs/2026-10-15-labbox-t2.9-review-fixes.txt`).
   Original note: `on_reply` drops a
   reply only if the word changed (`aria_segmentation_client.py:146`). "box", stop, "box" inside
   one SAM 3 call lets the first reply through, with the old image and gaze. Fix: a counter that
   `on_prompt` bumps on every word, captured in `segment`, compared in `on_reply` (about 4
   lines), plus one test for exactly that sequence. A counter, not a timestamp: nothing to
   compare but equality. The module docstring says so until the fix lands.
3. **Client test cleanup** (Dion agreed 2026-10-15). **Done 2026-10-15**, 15 tests now, pass on the box with item 2.
   Original note: In `test_aria_segmentation_client.py`:
   delete `test_a_word_sends_one_grasp_request` (line 130 already checks one request per word),
   merge the two "same word again" tests into one with both asserts and both reasons, move the
   `requests()` helper up with the others. 16 tests become 15 (14 plus item 2's new one).
   Run on the box together with item 2's new test. Section 3.3 should then say 15 here plus 6 in `test_gaze_pick.py`.

4. **Box check of the readiness fix** (Dion 2026-10-15). **PASS 2026-10-15**: "Both workers took
   their first frame after 67 s" before "Playing from", 4 of 4 words picked a box, no "No glasses
