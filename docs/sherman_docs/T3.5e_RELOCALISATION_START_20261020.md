# T3.5e restart-in-map localisation: start, 2026-10-20

Status: started. Offline feasibility only. Nothing here is a localisation result. T3.5e is not
passed. No robot time used. Not pushed.

## The premise (confirmed from the pinned source `[code]`)

The pinned FAST-LIVO2 fork only writes a PCD (`pcd_save.pcd_save_en`, saved on shutdown to
`Log/pcd/all_raw_points.pcd`). Nothing reads one back, so "restart in the map" needs an external
relocaliser that finds the transform from a fresh session's `camera_init` frame to the saved
map's frame. The bridge in T3.5f would then publish `map -> odom` from it. T3.5e therefore decides
what has to wrap the mapper, as its task text says.

## Decision: version 1 is option A `[decision, Sherman, 2026-10-20]`

The robot restarts near a known pose. An operator-supplied guess is refined by registration. Start
anywhere (global relocalisation) is version 2 and out of scope here.

## What was tried (offline, from the T3.5d bags; no robot, no mapper rerun)

`tools/fastlivo/reloc/reloc_offline_prototype.py`: build a map by transforming the hand-driven
bag's LiDAR scans with the mapper's own odometry (no deskew, no loop closure), voxelise at 0.10 m,
then register the first 3 s of the spin-corrected bag (a separate mapper launch, started at a different
position from the tape mark, per Sherman, attended T3.5d, 2026-10-20) against it with point-to-plane ICP in numpy and scipy. Only `extrinsic_T` is applied;
`extrinsic_R` is identity in the mapper config `[config: t35d config line 27, T3.5_LIDAR_IMU_EXTRINSIC_FINDING.md]`.
No Open3D or small_gicp on the box. Runs 1 to 3 are the first session; runs 4 to 7 (below) are
the T3.5e follow-up. Box run directory `~/rcp-Gappler/log/t35e-reloc/` (needs `install_nav` sourced).

### Correction to the first write-up

The first version said "the odometry-accumulated map is about 5 cm thick". That was wrong. The
5 cm figure is the point-to-nearest-point rms against a 0.10 m voxel map. That spacing alone gives
roughly 5 cm at a perfect fit, so it is the voxel floor, not a measure of the map. The
point-to-plane residual is not capped that way: it is 1.3 to 2.6 cm in the self-tests below
`[measured on this prototype]`. Map thickness is not otherwise measured `[inferred at most]`.

### Basin of convergence, reread with a tolerance that matches the noise (superseded: measured around the wrong answer)

"Same answer" now means within 10 cm and 4 deg of the identity-start answer (run 4, unfiltered).
Starts that land at 5.7 cm / 3.6 deg (offset 0, +0.3 m) belong to the same answer; the old 2 cm /
0.5 deg test counted them as failures. Reread with this tolerance, run 4: 5 of 17 starts match
strictly. Near misses at 13 cm / 3.2 deg (+5 deg), 6 cm / 4.1 deg (+10 deg), 13 cm / 3.3 deg (+15 deg)
sit just outside the tolerance, so the basin is better stated as a range:

- Yaw: roughly -10 to +15 deg. -15 deg fails (42 cm / 7.5 deg); +/-30 deg fails.
- Position: roughly 0.3 m along one axis. The 0.3 m diagonal (-0.3, -0.3) fails (1.16 m).
- Stated as roughly +/-15 deg and +/-0.3 m, decimetre-level `[measured on this prototype]`, with the
  two caveats above (-15 deg and the diagonal are outside). It is not the same under other
  settings: with the planarity filter the basin shrinks to 2 of 17 (run 5); with the 20 s map it
  widens to 10 of 17 (run 6) while landing somewhere else (1.95 m).

So the basin is a property of this map and filter, not a number to design around.
**Superseded:** this was measured against the identity-start answer, which the follow-up shows is a wrong
local fit. Use the corrected basin in the follow-up.

## Placement follow-up (T3.5e A, runs 9 to 20): the 1.2 m figure was wrong

Verdict, one of three: **placement difference real.** The two sessions did not start at the same pose. The
corrected offset of the spin-corrected start, in the hand-driven map frame, is about **x 1.94 m, y 1.19 m, yaw
within 1 deg** (2.3 m in plan) `[measured on this prototype]`. It is a LiDAR-geometry result with one
tape-measured check (see "First scored check" below), and no accuracy statistic.

### Why I now say real, not aliased or undetermined

The criteria I used: one dominant best fit well clear of the rivals, the same answer when the query length and the
filter change, and the camera agreeing on direction.

| Query | Filter | Polished origin pose (x, y m; yaw deg) | Inliers | Plane rms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| first 3 s | on | 1.94, 1.20; +0.07 | 0.975 | 2.7 cm |
| first 3 s | off | 1.94, 1.18; -0.90 | 0.974 | 2.5 cm |
| first 15 s | on | 1.93, 1.19; -1.16 | 0.959 | 3.0 cm |

`[measured]` (runs 16 to 18). The three agree within 1.5 cm and about 1.3 deg. The earlier sweep best,
(0.18 to 0.42, 1.22), scored 0.73 to 0.75 inliers and 3.9 cm, and every other distinct solution scored 0.70 or less
`[measured]` (runs 9, 13). A session against its own map scores 1.00 and 1.3 to 2.6 cm, so the new fit is close to
that quality. How I got there:

1. **Degeneracy.** At the old sweep best, `A^T A` (the 4-DOF least-squares curvature) had its weakest direction almost
   pure x (0.92 to 0.99 of tx), eigenvalue 0.010 against 0.95 for the strongest, condition 90 to 100, with the
   planarity filter on. 96% of the kept normals were vertical (floor and ceiling), so walls, which fix x and y, hardly
   entered. `[measured]` (runs 9, 10). With the filter off the condition is 17 to 18 and 10% and 13% of the normals
   point along x and y (run 13). x was the aliased axis.
2. **y fit profile** (`results/placement/yprofile_*.csv` and `.png`): for each y of the query centroid, x and yaw
   re-optimised, 14 starts including the sweep's top solutions. The profile is rough with several local dips
   (e.g. y = -1.8, -1.2, 0.4, 0.8, 1.6), but its best point is a clear winner: 0.975 inliers against 0.65 to 0.84
   elsewhere. The profile's y is the query centroid, not the origin, which is why the best row (y = -1.7) is the
   same pose as the origin y = 1.2 after converting (runs 14 to 18). I misread that conversion at first. `[measured]`
3. **Global sweep with a 15 s query** (run 15): the sweep still missed the best solution (its starts only reach 1.5 m)
   and put x at 0.2 to 0.4, y 1.2 to 1.6. The profile-then-ICP route found the 0.959 solution above. So the sweep needs
   wider offsets (this is a fix to the prototype, not to the data).
4. **Camera first frames** (`results/placement/f0_first.png` hand-driven start, `f1_first.png` spin-corrected start):
   same scene, shelf plus TV plus sofa, but the shelf is about 1.8 times wider from the second start, and moves from
   left of centre to right of centre. The camera looks along LiDAR +x (third row of `Rcl` is [1, 0, 0]) and
   +y is left, so "closer to the shelf and shifted left" matches a start 1.94 m forward and 1.19 m left in direction.
   `[inferred]`. I did not check the magnitude: the focal length was not used and a rough estimate gave about
   0.75 m of lateral shift, not a clean match. So the camera says "the views differ and the direction fits", nothing more.
5. **Raw range check** (run 11, first 1 s of each bag, 10th-percentile range by azimuth): inconclusive. The bins are
   30 deg apart with 15 deg windows, so they leave gaps, and the hand-driven start has something 0.5 to 0.9 m behind
   and to the left (probably the operator). Not used for the verdict.

| filter on (floor only, cond 90) | 8/8 at 0.3 and 0.5 m, 4/8 at 1.0 m, 2/8 at 1.5 m, 0/8 at 2.0 m | +/-5 deg only |

so the run files stay reproducible, and say so here.

Error is relocalised minus measured; distance error is the difference of the two distances from the tape mark.

- The error is mostly x, about 0.06 m against 0.03 m in y, and x is the axis the planarity filter starved (weakest
  direction of `A^T A`: eigenvalue 0.010 with the filter on, 0.158 with it off, both before the default change).
  The tape measurement is good to about 5 cm, so a 0.06 m error in x and a 0.067 m error in plan are at the size of
  the measurement's own error (two axes at 5 cm each combine to about 7 cm in plan). The check is therefore consistent
  with agreement within tolerance, and cannot say the registration is better than about 5 cm. `[inferred]` The
  filter-off rerun with the new default is running; the error will be reported again from it.
- What the error includes beyond the registration: the hand-driven start itself may not sit exactly on the tape mark
  or at the mark's heading. `[inferred]` The scored pose is relative to the hand-driven run's start, which is taken to be
  the tape mark.
- Reference point: the tape measured to the middle of the base, while the relocalised pose is where the mapper puts the
  sensor origin (the IMU position, about 0.19 m from the base in the T3.5f constant (0.191, 0.023, 0.156) m
  `[code: T3.5d result]`). Because the same sensor sits at the same place on the robot in both sessions, the fixed
  offset cancels in the difference of the two starts when the headings match. If the heading actually differed by
  theta, the leftover is about 0.19 m times theta: 1.7 cm at 5 deg, 3.3 cm at 10 deg. `[inferred]`
- Heading: the relocalised yaw (+0.07 deg, -0.90 deg and -1.16 deg in runs 16 to 18) agrees with "roughly the same"
  but nothing measured the heading, so it is a consistency, not a score.
- **This is one pose pair, not an accuracy statistic.** It shows the 2.3 m offset is right to within about 7 cm in this
  one case. The requirement of at least three floor marks with measured spacing and heading stands, and T3.5e is not
  done until recovery is shown against them.

### What the follow-up changes

- The earlier basin of "+/-15 deg, 0.3 m" is withdrawn. Option A with a guess within about 1 m and 20 deg is the
  working range, on this map and with walls included.
- Roll and pitch: levelling by the first pose (above) is still needed.
- Source and consistency: Sherman confirmed the spin test started at a different position from the tape mark
  (Sherman, attended T3.5d, 2026-10-20). The relocalised offset (1.94, 1.19 m, yaw about 0, 2.3 m) is consistent
  with that. The magnitude now has one tape-measured check (see "First scored check" below). An earlier draft of this
  note assumed both sessions began at the same tape mark; that assumption was wrong and is removed. The hand-driven
  run's own end pose (mapper 12 mm and 2.4 deg from its start, Sherman by eye about 1 cm from the mark,
  `T3.5d_ATTENDED_RESULT_20261020.md`) is optional colour only and is not used here.

## What a credible version needs

1. A better map: the mapper's own saved PCD (deskewed, voxelised) from a replay of the
   hand-driven bag with `pcd_save_en` on, written to the bind-mounted `Log` directory.
2. A better registration: GICP or NDT (PCL 1.12 is installed on the box) with a coarse global
   initialisation (yaw sweep plus scan descriptors) so an arbitrary start can work.
3. Independent ground truth: at least three floor marks with measured spacing and heading, one
   chosen as the map origin. T3.5e is only done when recovery is shown against those marks. **First check:** if the
   tape mark can be identified, measure the spin start position against it (distance along and across the mark,
   and heading). That is the first ground-truth test of the 2.3 m offset (1.94, 1.19 m).
4. Scope: version 1 = option A (decided above).

## Not proven

No relocalisation accuracy, no physical-reference result, no basin size beyond this prototype's
own map. The two sessions' true relative pose is unknown, so the prototype cannot be scored
against ground truth beyond one tape-measured pose pair (error about 7 cm in plan, not a statistic); the 2.3 m offset is a LiDAR-geometry result and the three-floor-mark check has not been done. The package in step 3 was not run.
T3.5e is not DONE.
