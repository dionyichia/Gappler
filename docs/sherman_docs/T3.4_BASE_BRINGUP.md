### Charging and handoff

The **ECHO Series Product Manual v1.8.7**, section 3.1.5 (page 11), requires chassis
shutdown/power-off and the dedicated charging port. Section 3.8 (page 19) requires the
charger voltage to match the chassis nameplate, supervised charging, no operation while
charging, and no continuous charge longer than eight hours; stop if the charger overheats.
The manual lists 220 V AC charger input and both approximately 29 V and 25 V charger
variants. It does **not** establish the correct output current/polarity for this unit.
Do not select a generic 24 V adapter from nominal battery voltage or use the CAN hub.
Chassis-nameplate and charger-label compatibility still need verification.
