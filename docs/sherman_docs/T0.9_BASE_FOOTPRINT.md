## Manufacturer dimensions

The supplied manual specifies the ECHO-PLUS chassis as `460 x 380 x 140 mm`, with a stated
`265 mm` radius of rotation and `35 mm` ground clearance (manual p. 6; dimensional drawing p. 8).

| Rated payload | 50 kg |

## Nav2 comparison

The shared task's `0.20 m` radius is smaller than the manual's `0.265 m` stated rotation radius by
`0.065 m`. It must not be treated as a conservative circular footprint.

The full `460 x 380 mm` rectangular envelope has a calculated corner radius of approximately
`0.298 m` if `mobile_base_link` is centred. The chassis has rounded corners, so this is a conservative
bound rather than a substitute for a physical measurement. Mounted arm, LiDAR, camera, or cable
hardware may increase the required footprint further.
