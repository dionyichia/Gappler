## Separate shutdown defect and limits

After the bag had finalized, the D455 exited cleanly but the LiDAR driver exited
**-7 (SIGBUS)** after reporting SDK deinitialization. The kernel journal records
a stack-segment trap inside `libspdlog.so.1.9.2`; that locates the observed trap,
not its root cause. Logs, journal and `shutdown-findings.txt` are preserved.
No driver restart, dependency change or automatic hardware reproduction was
attempted. All capture drivers, preview and recorder were confirmed stopped.

Offline bag integrity does not certify driver shutdown, calibrated extrinsics,
physical sensor synchronization, successful visual matching or moving-map
accuracy. F2 image selection/comparison, a new reviewed bag identity and corrected
checked config pair, and the shutdown investigation remain separate pre-replay
work. Frozen `t35b_proof3` configs stay unchanged. T3.5c remains in progress.
