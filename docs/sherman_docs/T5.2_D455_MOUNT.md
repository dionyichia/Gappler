# T5.2: D455 Mobile-Base Mount

**Shared task:** [`../dion_docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md`](../dion_docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md), T5.2.
**Record role:** Sherman-owned design evidence. The shared project plan remains authoritative for task
status.

## Objective

Design a forward-facing, rigid mount for an **Intel RealSense D455** on the Hexman Robotics
ECHO-PLUS mobile base. The shared task requires it to be clear of the arm's swept volume and not view
the robot's own body.

## Reported mechanical constraints

- `[reported]` The planned mount uses `20 x 20 mm` aluminum extrusion.
- `[reported]` The required extrusion length is `190 mm`.
- `[manual]` The ECHO-PLUS chassis is `460 x 380 x 140 mm`; see
  [`T0.9_BASE_FOOTPRINT.md`](T0.9_BASE_FOOTPRINT.md) for the manufacturer source and chassis envelope.

## Reported completed design

- `[reported]` Mount CAD and dimensions are complete as of 2026-09-16.

## Still required before fabrication

- `[unverified]` D455 USB 3 connection, live RGB-D stream, and cable routing.
- `[unverified]` Chassis attachment points and fastener specification.
- `[unverified]` Camera height, forward offset, and pitch.
- `[unverified]` Clearance from the rear-facing arm's swept volume.
- `[unverified]` Whether the mount or cable extends beyond the navigation footprint.

## Next steps

1. Test the provided D455 under T0.1 and record its serial number and cable type.
2. Photograph and measure the chassis attachment points.
3. Check the completed mount drawing against the D455 pose, fasteners, and cable route.
4. Check arm-clearance and self-occlusion before fabrication.
5. After fitting, measure the actual camera transform for T5.4 and update the integrated navigation
   footprint if necessary.
