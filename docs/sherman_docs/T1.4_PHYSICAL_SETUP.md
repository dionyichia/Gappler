## Objective and scope

T1.4 is the physical safety setup at the robot: clear working volume, stop button within reach,
mount and cable check. T1.4 gates T1.6 (first power, no motion commanded) — not T1.7 (first
commanded motion). Motion safety stays downstream with T1.3 (validated home pose), T1.6 (joint
feedback), and T1.7 (supervised move).

Because no physical table/cell exists yet, this plan splits T1.4 into a robot-side half that can
be done now and a cell half that stays blocked.

- **T1.4-now:** robot-side safety setup and as-built measurements for power-on readiness. No arm
  motion, no motion commanded.
- **T1.4-cell (blocked):** table geometry, grasp zone, wrist-camera view of objects, approach
  clearance. Required before any grasp-volume claim or real motion — not before power-on.

**Lean rule:** T1.4 DONE means safe for power-on with no motion, plus documented residuals. Sweep
bounds, wall-vs-volume proof, TF closure to the millimetre, Nav2 footprint closure, ROS/power-on
procedure, camera health, and D455 mount work belong to T1.3/T1.5/T1.6/T1.7/T0.9/T5.x — not to
T1.4 DONE.

## Ground rules

- Arm stays unpowered. Joints stay untouched. The as-found posture is arm straight up.
- Forcing braked joints can damage motors, gearboxes, brakes, or encoders. The vendor driver
  reports joint-brake state (`arm/vendor/rm_driver/include/rm_driver/rm_interface.h:449`), and its
  error table names "joint brake is not opened".
- Camera work is camera-only (USB power, no arm motion), with shared-hardware checks first.
- No table/cell means no grasp-view claim and no final home-pose claim.

## B. Parked-state envelope (structure-first containment + heights)

Section B verifies one claim: the entire top structure — extrusion, computer, PSU, LiDAR body,
mast base, cameras, cables, and the arm mount/flange — sits inside the platform's length × width.
The chassis edge is the datum (the fixed, manufacturer-defined reference plane), not the subject:
sight along each edge plane to prove the structure crosses none of them. Edge-sighting proves both
size and placement at once; measuring the structure's own footprint alone would prove size but miss
an off-center overhang, which is why the old structure-footprint row lives here and nowhere else.

Split the arm in two for this section: the flange and mount are static, contained, and checked in
B1 like any other hardware. The arm's links in motion are dynamic, excluded from B1, and gated by
T1.7 — reaching the table requires leaving the footprint by design (see Parked vs working
envelopes below).

## Parked vs working envelopes (first purpose requirement for T1.3)

The arm must leave the footprint to reach a table — that is the job, not an edge case. So there
are two envelopes:

- **Parked envelope (this section's subject):** the robot as it sits. Everything static inside
  x-y, verified by B1–B3. This is the transport/idle safety claim.
- **Working envelope (derived, never taped by hand):** arm extended toward the table by design.
  Bounded later via model/FK sweep from home to grasp, checked against table and obstacles with
  margins, gated by T1.7.

Purpose requirement this establishes for the T1.3 decision: home should be a **tucked posture
inside the footprint** — the parked robot stays compact and safe (which also minimizes the volume
that must be cleared for unprompted homing at startup), and extension happens only during
commanded grasp motions. This is testable in FK per candidate row, not a vibe: elbow branches that
fold inward pass, ones that sprawl fail.

Consequence for the containment gate: the old flat G4 splits in two. **G4a** — home links
contained in B, evaluated height-aware (links below obstacle/table height must be inside B; links
above clear height allowed subject to a stated clearance floor, since flat x-y would fail a good
home for harmless high overhang). **G4b** — working sweep clearing table/obstacles by margin,
derived via model/FK, T1.7-gated. Per-row tuck verdicts come from sim FK, never from reasoning.

## How these feed T1.3

- Containment + max radius support (not prove) later safety-wall checks against the real cleared volume; the wall-vs-volume proof belongs to the T1.3 candidate / T1.7 preconditions. Home containment is evaluated height-aware (G4a); working sweep clearance is derived, never taped (G4b).
- Flange offsets/yaw test the TF (transform tree) mount assumptions behind every FK, approach, and
  wall comparison.
- Heights + as-found posture set vertical bounds and the reference state.
- Camera identity + FOV reference predict whether a candidate home can see a future grasp zone.
- Joint limits + the joint4 failure history supply the margin math for screening candidates.
- Photos + retained-largest values make the derivation auditable.
