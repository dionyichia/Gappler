# T3.5f live runbook: option 2 chain, SLAM Toolbox mapping every session

Prepared 2026-10-20. Protocol only: nothing run, no hardware touched, no result claimed. Operator: Sherman, attended.
No motion command is sent by anyone. The base is pushed by hand. Never publish `/cmd_vel`, `/goal_pose` or an arm topic.
Tags: `[code]` read from source, `[unverified]` expected but not yet seen live.

- [ ] Hardware not in use by someone else: `ps -eo pid,user,etime,args | grep -iE "realsense|rs_launch|ros2 launch|livox|Runner.Worker"`, `fuser /dev/video*`. Anyone there: stop, ask.

The mapper container defaults to 174 and the nav stack to 91, so a mixed setup sees no mapper pose. Gate: `ros2 topic list` shows only `/parameter_events` and `/rosout`.
- [ ] Mapper config: the corrected visual and camera YAML are staged in `docs/sherman_docs/tools/fastlivo/live/config` (`t35c_stationary02_20261019_visual.yaml`, `t35c_stationary02_20261019_camera.yaml`, byte-identical to `replay/config/`, extrinsic `[-0.011, -0.02329, 0.04412]`; a test pins both). Never use the pre-correction T3.5d contrast file for a live run (it is in the same folder, kept as evidence). `[code]`
- [ ] `T35_USE_SIM_TIME=false`. The script defaults to `true`, which is right for a bag and wrong live. `[code]`
- [ ] Robot level, on the tape mark, e-stop within reach, arm stowed.

### Checks at each step

1. **One publisher per TF edge.** `ros2 topic info /tf -v` and `/tf_static -v`. Expected owners `[code]`:
   - `odom` to `robot_base_link`: `fastlivo_odom_bridge` only. A second publisher means the base driver `pub_tf` is not off: stop.
   - `map` to `odom`: `slam_toolbox` only.
   - `robot_base_link` to `livox_frame` (`base_to_livox`) and to the arm frame (`robot_base_to_arm`): static, one each.
   - `camera_init` to `aft_mapped` must NOT be on `/tf`. It must be on `/fastlivo/tf`. If it shows on `/tf`, the remap is missing: stop the container.
   - `ros2 run tf2_tools view_frames`: one tree, no frame with two parents.
2. **Rates** (`ros2 topic hz`), all `[unverified]` live except where T3.5d measured:
   - `/livox/lidar` 10 Hz, `/livox/imu` about 200 Hz, `/cloud_relay` 10 Hz, `/scan` 10 Hz.
   - `/aft_mapped_to_init` about 20 Hz (measured T3.5d, two poses per scan).
   - bridge `odom` to `robot_base_link` about 10 Hz (final pose per scan only).
   - `map` to `odom` about 50 Hz (`transform_publish_period` 0.02).
3. **Stationary 30 s before any push.** Mapper pose moves under 1 cm (T3.5d: 5.7 mm in 30 s). Bridge log has no `max_startup_tilt_deg` or `max_jump_m` refusal. `/map` is not empty.
4. **Bridge stale stop, at the end of the session.** After the final push, leave the base still and stop the mapper container with Ctrl-C. Within 0.5 s the bridge must log `no mapper pose for 0.50 s: publishing nothing on odom -> robot_base_link until it returns`, and `odom` to `robot_base_link` must stop updating (`ros2 run tf2_ros tf2_echo odom robot_base_link` freezes or errors). A mapper abort (`RCLError ... context is invalid`) on stop is the known T3.5d teardown, not a new fault. `[code]` bridge, `[unverified]` live.

## 2. Push-drive procedure

- Hands on the base, walking pace or slower, smooth start and stop. No sudden stops, no wheel spin.
- Gentle turns: wide arcs, a full turn takes 10 s or more (T3.5d: slow turn about 360 deg per run). No snap rotations.
- Stay in LiDAR view of walls and furniture. Avoid long glass or mirror runs (T3.5d: those sections mapped badly).
- Keep a loop short: about 30 m path, farthest point about 3.5 m (T3.5d scale).
- Do not stop for more than a few seconds mid-run with people crossing the LiDAR view.
- **End exactly on the tape mark**, same heading as the start. Stand still 10 s. Read the mapper pose and the bridge `odom` to `robot_base_link` position. Return-to-start error is the headline number (T3.5d mapper: 12 mm horizontal, 2.4 deg yaw).
- Watch RViz: if the 2D map doubles a wall or the odom jumps, note the time and keep going to the mark. Do not stop to fix it.
- Then step 4 of the checks (stale stop), then shut down in reverse order, each process by its own group (`kill -INT -<pgid>`), no `pkill`.

## 4. Not proven by this runbook

Frame ownership and rates are expectations from source. Nothing here shows Nav2 can follow the resulting `map` frame, the `map` to `odom` jump behaviour under loop closure, or accuracy beyond one tape-mark return.
