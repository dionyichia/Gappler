## Objective

Close the remaining T0.1 gaps for the provided base D455: USB 3 link, live RGB-D stream, and
ROS-driver delivery of color, depth, and aligned depth.

## Setup

Both RealSense cameras on direct root-hub USB 3 ports at 5000M (the D455 needed a port/cable swap
off a 480M USB 2 path; the D435i moved to its own direct port so no hub is shared):

| Camera | USB path | Speed | Serial (driver) |
|---|---|---|---|
| D435i (wrist) | Bus 002, direct | 5000M | `251843061155` |
| D455 (base) | Bus 002, direct | 5000M | `146222253541` |

`[observed]` The driver-reported serials differ from the sysfs `ID_SERIAL_SHORT` values. The
driver's numbers above are authoritative (from `realsense2_camera` device discovery).

## Method

Launched only the camera driver on an isolated ROS domain (no arm, base, or grasp processes):

```bash
ROS_DOMAIN_ID=88 ROS_LOCALHOST_ONLY=1 ros2 launch realsense2_camera rs_launch.py \
  "serial_no:='146222253541'" align_depth.enable:=true
```

Two launch facts worth recording:

- `serial_no` must be passed as a quoted string. An all-digit serial is parsed as an integer and
  the node dies with `parameter 'serial_no' has invalid type`.
- The launch must be detached from the SSH session (tmux on the box). Foreground launches died
  with SIGINT when the SSH session ended.

Driver profiles: Depth Z16 `848x480@30`, Color RGB8 `1280x720@30`. Warmed up 60 s past
`RealSense Node Is Up`, then recorded all three topics simultaneously for 60 s with
`ros2 bag record`. Driver stopped afterwards; box left with no ROS processes.

## Result

`ros2 bag info`, 74.8 s window, 6507 messages total:

| Topic | Count | Sustained rate |
|---|---|---|
| `/camera/camera/color/image_raw` | 2117 | 28.3 Hz |
| `/camera/camera/depth/image_rect_raw` | 2273 | 30.4 Hz |
| `/camera/camera/aligned_depth_to_color/image_raw` | 2117 | 28.3 Hz |

Bag was 10.8 GiB and was deleted after analysis; counts above are the record.

## Rate-fluctuation note (resolved)

`[observed]` Live `ros2 topic hz` readings swung 6–30 Hz and decayed across samples while the box
was idle (load 0.39, 52 C package temp, driver 16.7% CPU, zero driver warnings). The bag counts
above prove sustained ~30 Hz delivery on all three streams over 75 s, so the `hz` swings were a
**measurement artifact** (sequential topics, short sliding windows, startup transients), not a
stream fault. Prefer fixed-window bag counts over `hz` snapshots for rate claims.

## Interpretation

`[observed]` T0.1 is complete for the D455: USB 3 link, live RGB and depth streams, and ROS-driver
color, depth, and aligned-depth delivery at ~30 Hz sustained. Remaining camera work (mount
fabrication T5.3, LiDAR-camera calibration T5.5) is unchanged.
